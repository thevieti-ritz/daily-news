How to play

Match the suit or rank of the top card to play. If you can't play, draw a card from the pile.

8 — Skip: the other player misses their turn (you go again).
2 — Draw Two: opponent draws 2 cards, unless they also play a 2 to stack it higher.
Queen — Reverse: with only 2 players, this acts like a skip — you go again.

First player to play all their cards wins!
